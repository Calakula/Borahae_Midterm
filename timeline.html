The debut

On June 13, 2013 the seven members debut with the single album 2 Cool 4 Skool.

022014

First studio album

Dark & Wild arrives in August as the group's first full-length Korean album.

032015–16

The Most Beautiful Moment

The two-part "youth" series and Wings bring BTS a much wider audience around the world.

042017–18

Love Yourself

The Love Yourself series carries a message of self-love and goes global.

052020

Dynamite

The English-language single "Dynamite" is released, followed by the album BE in November.

062022

Proof

The anthology album Proof celebrates nine years and the group focuses on solo projects.

072025

All members complete their military service, and ARMY waits for the next chapter.
