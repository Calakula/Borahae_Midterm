The story so far

A timeline of moments

Scroll down — each moment glows into view.

2013

The debut

On June 13, 2013 the seven members debut with the single album 2 Cool 4 Skool.

2014

First studio album

Dark & Wild arrives in August as the group's first full-length Korean album.

2015–16

The Most Beautiful Moment

The two-part "youth" series and Wings bring BTS a much wider audience around the world.

2017–18

Love Yourself

The Love Yourself series carries a message of self-love and goes global.

2020

Dynamite

The English-language single "Dynamite" is released in August 2020, followed by the album BE in November.

2022

Proof

The anthology album Proof celebrates nine years, and the group announces a break to focus on solo projects.

2025

All seven back

By mid-2025 all members have completed their military service in South Korea, and ARMY waits for the next chapter.
